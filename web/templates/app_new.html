2. The scheme

Kagaz fetches this one page directly — no login, no crawling, no submitting. Works best on a direct PDF link or a plain notification page.

Many portals build their pages with JavaScript, which a direct fetch can't run — Kagaz will tell you if it got nothing readable. A screenshot is the more reliable option for those.

PNG or JPEG, and a clear phone photo of a printed notice works too. A notification rarely fits on one screen — select every screenshot at once (Ctrl/Cmd -click, or drag a selection) and Kagaz reads them in order as one document.
