Three things Kagaz does

1

Holds your documents and watches their dates

Income certificate, caste certificate, domicile, marksheet, passbook, photo, signature — stored once. The ones that expire are tracked, and Kagaz tells you which to renew before a deadline turns it into a problem.

2

Prepares your submission for a specific form

Give it the form. It reads what that form requires, then produces a sheet of your details laid out to copy into each field, and your documents converted to exactly what that form asks for — resized to its pixel and KB limits, JPG turned into PDF where that's what's wanted. Download the whole folder.

3

Tells you what would get it rejected

Your name spelled differently across two documents, a date of birth that doesn't match, a certificate that expires before the deadline, a family income above that form's stated limit. And the things no tool can check — like whether your bank account is NPCI-seeded — listed plainly so you check them yourself instead of assuming they passed.

However you found it, Kagaz reads it. Nothing typed in by hand.

Paste a link
The form's webpage or PDF URL. Kagaz reads that one page.
Upload a screenshot
A phone photo or screenshot of the notice or portal page.
Upload the PDF
The official notification, straight from the department's site.
Paste the text
Copy the eligibility and document rules out of any page.

Every rule comes from the form you give it — document list, size and format limits, deadline, income ceiling. Nothing is assumed from any other scheme.

What lands in your download

A sheet of details to copy in

Every field that form asks for, next to the value read off your own documents — so you copy instead of re-typing, and the spelling matches what you uploaded. Fields Kagaz can't source are left blank rather than guessed.

Documents in the right size and format

Resized to the pixel dimensions and KB limit that form states, and converted between formats where it asks for one you don't have — a JPG scan turned into the PDF it wants. Your originals in the locker are never altered.

A rejection checklist, split honestly

What Kagaz checked and found — name and date-of-birth mismatches across documents, expiry against the deadline, income against that form's limit — kept separate from what it cannot check, so nothing unverified is dressed up as verified.

An audit report, and your decisions on it

When something is genuinely ambiguous, Kagaz stops and asks you — one finding at a time — and records what you decided and why. It never edits a value on your behalf, and it never submits anything to any portal.
